## Scenario

"Maya was really deep into the cylinder blocks and I got excited and said 'Wow, you're doing such a great job with those!' She looked up, smiled at me, and then wandered off to do something else. I think I ruined it."

## Interpretation

Yes — the praise interrupted the cycle. This is the failure mode Montessori names most often. Maya was working in private absorption; the praise reframed the work as a performance for mother's approval, and once the performance was acknowledged, the work was done. Not catastrophic — Maya will concentrate again — but if the pattern repeats, she will learn to work for approval rather than from inner direction.

What the parent can do differently next time: silent observation. Share the delight in your journal or with your partner later, not with the child mid-work.

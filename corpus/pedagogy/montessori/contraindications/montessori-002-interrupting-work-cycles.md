## Reasoning

Montessori's most absolute prohibition: do not interrupt a child in concentrated work. Not for a meal that can wait, not for a photograph, not for a visitor, not for praise. The work cycle is near-sacred in the tradition — concentration is the developmental event itself, and breaking it discards the very thing the environment exists to produce.

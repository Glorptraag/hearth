## Text

John Holt's central insight, developed over two decades of close observation in conventional classrooms before he turned to homeschooling, was that children learn most when they are most trusted to direct their own learning, and least when fear of being wrong replaces curiosity as their primary engagement with knowledge. He came to argue that what schools called "teaching" was largely getting in the way of a learning capacity children already possessed.

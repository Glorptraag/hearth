## Text

A survey of 75 adults who had been unschooled as children found that 83% had pursued some form of higher education and 44% had completed or were in bachelor's degree programs. The great majority reported being very satisfied with their unschooling upbringing. Only 3 of the 75 reported being unhappy, and all three were from family situations marked by depression and disengagement rather than by the unschooling approach itself.

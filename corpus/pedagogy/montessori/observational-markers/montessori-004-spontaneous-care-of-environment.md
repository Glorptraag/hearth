## What it indicates

When care-of-environment becomes spontaneous, the child has internalised the principle — he is no longer performing the exercise, the exercise has become him. Practical life behaviour extending beyond the formally presented exercises into ordinary moments is a high-weight developmental marker for a maturing Montessori home.

## Look for

- Child, unprompted, wipes up his own spill
- Child returns a material to its shelf without being asked
- Child picks up a dropped object that isn't his
- Child straightens a rug or tidies a space on his own initiative
- Practical life behaviour appears outside the formally presented exercises, in ordinary moments

## Scenario

"Finn started bashing the pink tower cubes against each other instead of stacking them. I didn't know what to do."

## Interpretation

Clear material misuse scenario. The right response: remove the cubes with one short sentence ("The cubes are not for bashing. They go back on the shelf."), return them to the shelf, offer an alternative. If Finn persists in bashing whenever the pink tower is out, it's a poor match right now — withdraw the material from rotation for a week or two and try again later.

What to notice next: is Finn misusing other materials too, or only this one? If only the pink tower, it's a specific mismatch. If across multiple materials, he may need more gross-motor release before fine-motor work will land.

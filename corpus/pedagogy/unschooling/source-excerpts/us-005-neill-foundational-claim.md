## Text

A. S. Neill, founder of Summerhill School in 1921, built his entire approach around a single conviction: that children are not naturally bad and do not need to be made good through fear, reward, or correction. His proposition was that a child raised in genuine freedom — including the freedom to refuse instruction — would, given a community of trusted adults and other children, develop into a person fundamentally at peace with themselves and capable of self-government. Summerhill operated on this premise for over a century and continues to do so.

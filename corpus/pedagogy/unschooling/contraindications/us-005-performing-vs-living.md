## Reasoning

Multiple practitioners have noted that the most common failure mode of unschooling is not the child but the parent — specifically, the parent who has adopted the language and identity of unschooling but is still operating from the anxiety and control patterns that unschooling is meant to dissolve. The child experiences the gap between the language and the reality.

Honest status: this is a contraindication directed at the parent's inner state rather than at any specific practice. It shows up as families who say all the right things but whose children are anxious, compliant in school-shaped ways, or visibly performing for the parent.

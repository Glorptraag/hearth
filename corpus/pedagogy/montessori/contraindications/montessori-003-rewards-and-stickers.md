## Reasoning

Montessori rejects rewards and punishments equally as distortions of natural motivation. The child's satisfaction is meant to come from the mastery itself and from the material's own control of error, not from adult approval.

Implication for Hearth: a family operating with a Montessori lens should not see badge-earning framed as external approval. The badge itself is fine; the framing must honour the child's own satisfaction of mastery, not the adult's praise reaction.

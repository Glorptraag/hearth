## Scenario

"Strangest morning. Both kids just… worked. Emma did the sandpaper letters for 25 minutes then moved to the movable alphabet. Theo did the knobbed cylinders three times in a row. I barely said a word. It felt like I wasn't doing anything."

## Interpretation

The parent's "not doing anything" is the most advanced Montessori facilitation there is. Two children in their own work cycles, no adult interference, mutual respect of concentration — this is the environment functioning. The feeling that she "wasn't doing" is the feeling of the method working.

What to notice next: this is the baseline to build toward, not the exception to be startled by. Over weeks, count the number of such mornings — they should become more common as the children internalise the work patterns and the environment matures.

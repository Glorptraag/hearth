## What's there now

`src/app/api/report/export/route.ts` builds the regulator-facing PDF (HEU
compliance report for QLD/SA/NT; learning report for NSW/VIC/WA/TAS/ACT). The
only standing text outside the data tables is the per-page footer
(route.ts ~L567–580):

```
Hearth LMS — {config.reportScreenTitle} — {learner.name} — Page i of N
```

There is **no review or disclaimer line**. A parent can generate the PDF and hand
it straight to a regulator with nothing on the page telling them — or the
regulator — that it's a self-compiled, AI-assisted draft they're responsible for
checking.

## Why this matters (who we're writing for)

> ⚠️ The handoff pointed to `docs/hearth-pilot-personas-v1.md` and
> `docs/hearth-parent-journey-v1.md` — **neither exists in the repo** (pointers
> drifted). Voice and audience are grounded instead in the canonical
> `docs/test-family-onboarding-packet.md` and `CLAUDE.md` voice rules.

The onboarding packet already sets the exact expectation we want the PDF to
honour (packet L17):

> "Reports the app generates (HEU-style PDFs, capability summaries) are designed
> to **support your compliance work, not replace your judgement**. Cross-check
> before submitting to a regulator…"

The pilot is 10–20 QLD-leaning families submitting to a real Home Education Unit.
The disclaimer protects them (they stay the author of record), protects Hearth
(no implied affiliation or official endorsement), and matches a promise we've
already made in writing. It also lands during the highest-stakes moment in the
product — handing a document to a government body.

## Voice constraints applied

Australian English, contractions, warm + plain, no hype, no freemium framing.
Reassure ("this supports your work"), don't alarm. Jurisdiction-aware via the
existing `config` object (`regulatoryBody`, `regulatoryBodyShort`) so the line
reads correctly in every state, not just QLD.

## Wording options

### Option A — Minimal (one sentence, page 1)

> Generated by Hearth from the entries you've logged. Please review it before you
> submit it to {regulatoryBodyShort}.

*Shortest, lowest-friction. Says "you review it" but doesn't flag the AI-assisted
mapping or the no-affiliation point.*

### Option B — Moderate **(recommended)**

> This report is compiled from the learning moments you've logged. Curriculum
> alignments are AI-assisted suggestions to support your record-keeping — not
> official determinations. Please review everything before you submit it to
> {regulatoryBody} ({regulatoryBodyShort}).

*Covers the three things that matter: (1) it's built from the parent's own data,
(2) the curriculum/capability mapping is AI-assisted and not authoritative,
(3) the parent reviews and submits. Warm, accurate, not alarming. This is the one
I'd ship.*

### Option C — Fuller (page-1 block, ~3 sentences)

> This report is compiled from the learning moments you've logged in Hearth.
> Subject and curriculum alignments are generated with AI assistance to support
> your record-keeping; they're suggestions, not official determinations. Hearth
> isn't affiliated with {regulatoryBody}, and you remain responsible for the
> content you submit — please review it in full first.

*Most defensive (explicit no-affiliation + "you remain responsible"). Heavier,
slightly more legalistic — better if legal wants the affiliation disclaimer
spelled out. Worth asking legal whether the "you remain responsible" line is
desirable or over-reaching.*

## Recommendation

**Option B**, placed as a wrapped italic line on **page 1** directly under the
"Generated {date}" line, in the existing muted grey. Rationale: it's the moment
the parent first sees the document, it's accurate about the AI-assisted mapping
(which the data tables lean on heavily), and it mirrors the onboarding-packet
promise almost word for word.

Open questions for Drew + legal:

1. Is the explicit **no-affiliation** statement (Option C) wanted for the pilot,
   or is that overkill for 10–20 known families? (Recommend deferring it unless
   legal asks.)
2. Should the line **also** repeat in the per-page footer (in case pages are
   separated)? Cheap to add; I've noted the variant below.
3. "official determinations" vs "official assessments" vs "an official
   curriculum mapping" — pick the phrase legal is most comfortable with.

## Staged code change (recommended Option B)

Inserted right after the "Generated {date}" header (route.ts ~L137–138). Uses
`splitTextToSize` so it wraps, and the existing muted grey + italic so it reads
as a note, not a headline.

```ts
  doc.setFontSize(11);
  doc.setFont('helvetica', 'normal');
  doc.text(`Generated ${format(now, 'd MMMM yyyy')}`, pageW / 2, y, { align: 'center' });
  y += 8;

  // ── Review / disclaimer note (cr-report-disclaimer) ──
  // WORDING PENDING DREW + LEGAL SIGN-OFF — DO NOT SHIP AS-IS.
  doc.setFontSize(8);
  doc.setFont('helvetica', 'italic');
  doc.setTextColor(128, 128, 128);
  const disclaimer =
    `This report is compiled from the learning moments you've logged. ` +
    `Curriculum alignments are AI-assisted suggestions to support your record-keeping — not official determinations. ` +
    `Please review everything before you submit it to ${config.regulatoryBody} (${config.regulatoryBodyShort}).`;
  const disclaimerLines = doc.splitTextToSize(disclaimer, pageW - 36);
  doc.text(disclaimerLines, pageW / 2, y, { align: 'center' });
  y += disclaimerLines.length * 4 + 6;
  doc.setTextColor(0, 0, 0);
  doc.setFont('helvetica', 'normal');
```

## Scenario

"Oliver (age 4) spent 35 minutes this morning with the button frame. He buttoned and unbuttoned the same frame maybe 20 times. I kept wanting to ask if he wanted to do something else but he looked so focused I just let him be. Eventually he put it back on the shelf and went to get a snack without a word."

## Interpretation

Textbook extended work cycle. The 35 minutes and 20 repetitions are not "obsessive" — they are the exact shape of a Montessori concentration cycle. The mother's instinct to let him be was correct and hard-won. The wordless return to the snack is the satisfied completion.

What the parent did well: protected the concentration; did not praise at the end.

What to notice next: over the coming weeks, watch whether Oliver seeks similar extended cycles with other practical life materials. Concentration is transferrable; it is the habit being built, not the buttoning.

## Reasoning

Montessori's freedom is structured freedom within a prepared environment. "Just letting the kids play" in an unprepared space is not Montessori — it is closer to Unschooling. Montessori would argue that the child left without prepared materials defaults to sensory overload rather than purposeful work, and that the adult's preparation of the environment is precisely what distinguishes the two traditions.

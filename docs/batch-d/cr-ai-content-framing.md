## What's there now

AI "Journey Observations" and post-save reflections are shown to parents with
**no indication they're machine-written**, and there's **no handling for First
Nations / religious / cultural / otherwise tender material**.

Two surfaces present AI output to parents:

1. **Portfolio** — `src/app/(auth)/our-story/portfolio/page.tsx` (~L881–886).
   A Journey Observation renders in an ember-tinted block with a ✨ "Journey
   Observation" eyebrow and the AI text in italic serif. Nothing says it's AI.

2. **Post-save surface** — `src/components/logger/PostSaveSurface.tsx`. After a
   save, the heading reads "Saved — and here's what we noticed." and
   `EnrichedBody` shows the AI `reflection` (`insight_suggestions[0]` or
   `journey_observation.text`). The "we" reads as Hearth-the-product, not "an AI
   model produced this."

The underlying text is generated by Anthropic Haiku at write-time (see
`src/lib/ai/enrich.ts` `SYSTEM_PROMPT`). The privacy page **does** disclose AI
processing (`src/app/(public)/privacy/page.tsx` §4), but that disclosure never
travels to the surfaces where the parent actually reads the output.

## Why this matters (who we're writing for)

> ⚠️ The handoff's persona/journey docs
> (`docs/hearth-pilot-personas-v1.md`, `docs/hearth-parent-journey-v1.md`)
> **don't exist** in the repo — pointers drifted. Audience/voice grounded in
> `docs/test-family-onboarding-packet.md` + `CLAUDE.md`.

Two reasons, both real for the pilot:

1. **Trust through honesty.** The onboarding packet (L17, L39) already frames
   Hearth's outputs as support for the parent's judgement, and tells families the
   AI "maps subjects and capability threads from a few honest sentences." Calling
   the reflection what it is — an AI starting point — keeps that promise at the
   point of reading. It also lowers the stakes: an AI "starting point" that
   misreads a moment is forgivable; "what Hearth noticed" presented as fact is
   not.

2. **Care for tender material.** Australian homeschoolers include First Nations
   families, families schooling for religious reasons, and families logging
   grief, disability, faith practice, and cultural knowledge. An AI reflection
   that flattens a smoking ceremony into "great science inquiry," or reframes a
   scripture lesson in secular developmental language, is a real harm vector. We
   can't make Haiku culturally fluent at the prompt level for the pilot
   (see cr-cultural-framework), but we *can* be honest that the parent is the
   authority and invite them to trust their own read. That's cheap, kind, and
   correct.

## Voice constraints applied

Australian English, contractions, warm, plain. Philosophy-neutral (a faith
family and an unschooling family should both feel respected). No alarm, no
hedging-to-death. Reassure, don't lecture. Keep it short enough not to nag.

---

## (a) Honest AI label — wording options

A small, persistent caption on AI insight blocks.

- **A1 (recommended):** "Drawn by Hearth's AI from this entry — a starting point
  for your reflection, not a verdict."
- **A2 (shorter):** "An AI starting point, drawn from your entry."
- **A3 (tag only):** an "AI" pill beside the ✨ eyebrow, with A2 as a tooltip.

*Recommend A1 on the post-save surface (room to breathe, shown once) and A2 on
the portfolio block (it repeats down a list, so keep it tight).*

## (b) Care note — wording options

Shown where AI insights are introduced; **not** repeated on every historical
entry (that would nag).

- **B1 (recommended):** "You know your child best. Where a moment touches on
  culture, faith, or something tender, trust your read over ours."
- **B2 (broader):** "These notes can't see the whole picture — especially around
  culture, faith, or family. Your judgement is the one that counts."
- **B3 (most explicit):** "Hearth's AI doesn't carry cultural or religious
  context. For moments that hold that kind of meaning, your words matter more
  than ours."

*Recommend B1: warm, concrete, names the categories without singling any out,
and reinforces parent authority. B3 is the most honest about the limitation if
Drew wants to be explicit.*

## Reasoning

Mason names this directly as one of the great evils of modern education. The child needs space and silence in which to wonder and grow. A parent who cannot stop talking is, however lovingly, crowding out the inner life the child is trying to form.

Tension with other traditions: Almost all of Hearth’s supported pedagogies share some version of this concern — Montessori has the “silent period” principle, Waldorf emphasises restrained adult presence, Unschooling values not-directing. Mason is the most explicit about the role of silence as an active educational instrument.

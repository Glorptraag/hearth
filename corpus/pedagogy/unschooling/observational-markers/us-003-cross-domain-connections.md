## What it indicates

Unschooled children frequently form connections across domains in ways that schooled children, organised by subject silos, often do not. A child who connects what they noticed about insects to what they read in a fantasy novel to what they saw at the museum is demonstrating the kind of integrated thinking the unschooling tradition most prizes — not because it was instructed, but because it arose from the child's own meaning-making.

## Look for

- Child spontaneously references something from one domain while engaged in another
- Child notices similarities or contrasts across topics they have encountered separately
- Child applies a concept from a video game or book to a real-world situation
- Child asks integrative questions ("is that the same as the thing in...")
- Child's vocabulary is unusually broad for their age in domains they care about

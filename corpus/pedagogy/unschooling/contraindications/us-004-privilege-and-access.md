## Reasoning

Honest unschooling writers, including Akilah Richards and Jody Allard, have noted that successful unschooling typically depends on at least one parent being available much of the time, on a literacy-rich home environment, on access to community and resources, on financial stability, and often on cultural capital that schools normally transmit. Families without these conditions can still unschool, but the approach does not transcend material circumstances.

Honest status: this is increasingly recognised within the movement and should not be hidden in Hearth's framing. The Australian context is more accessible than the US in some respects (the homeschool community is well-organised; registration is accessible) but the privilege critique still applies.

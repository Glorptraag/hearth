## What it indicates

When a child returns to a topic, activity, or skill across days and weeks under their own initiative, with no parental prompting or reward, this is the unschooling tradition's strongest single indicator that authentic learning is happening. The duration matters less than the unprompted return. A child who plays the same game for two weeks then stops is showing the pattern just as much as a child who reads dinosaur books for six months — both are pursuing without coercion.

## Look for

- Child returns to a topic or activity unprompted across multiple days
- Child asks questions about it that go beyond surface curiosity
- Child seeks out additional material (books, videos, conversations, places) related to it
- Child incorporates it into play, conversation, or making
- Child's engagement is not contingent on a parent's presence or approval
- The interest may end abruptly — this is normal and not a failure

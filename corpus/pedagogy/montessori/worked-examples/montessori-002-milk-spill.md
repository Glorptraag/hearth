## Scenario

"Lily (3) insisted on pouring her own milk and spilled most of it. I wanted to grab the jug but I held back. She got a cloth from the drawer and wiped it up herself, then poured again, more carefully this time, and got most of it in the cup."

## Interpretation

Practical life working exactly as designed. The spill is not failure — it is feedback. Lily used the material's own control of error to refine her movement on the second attempt. The cloth in the drawer is the prepared environment doing its job.

What to notice next: Lily is in a sensitive period for fine-motor precision. Offer pouring activities of graduated difficulty — small jug to small cup, water first, then rice, then grain, then milk. Keep the cloth accessible.

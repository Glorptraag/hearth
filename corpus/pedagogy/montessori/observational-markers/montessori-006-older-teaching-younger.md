## What it indicates

This is Montessori's explicit argument for mixed-age groupings realised. The older child consolidates his own learning by teaching; the younger receives a model closer to his stage than any adult can provide. Spontaneous peer teaching is the signature of a mature Montessori community and should never be prompted — only noticed.

## Look for

- An older sibling notices a younger one struggling and, without adult prompting, demonstrates the activity
- The demonstration is often silent — modelling by doing it slowly
- The older child does not take over; he models and withdraws
- The younger child returns to the task with the modelled refinement

## Scenario

"Getting dressed this morning was a disaster. Noah (2.5) wanted to do every button himself and we were going to be late. I tried to help with the last button and he completely lost it — crying, pushing my hand away, shouting 'no, me do it.' I ended up letting him finish even though we were 20 minutes late to playgroup."

## Interpretation

Noah's storm is the fierce defence of his developmental work. At 2.5 he is in a strong sensitive period for dressing independence, and adult hands on the last button crash directly into that work. The correct response was exactly what the mother eventually arrived at: let him finish.

Practical adjustment: budget 20 extra minutes for dressing in the morning. This is not "delay" — it is the developmental work of the day happening before playgroup.

## Text

Across families practising unschooling, children who were never formally taught to read learned to do so anywhere from age 4 to age 11 or later. The mean age at which fluent reading emerged in one survey was approximately 8.4 years. By the time these children reached age 15, those who had read early and those who had read late were indistinguishable in their reading capacity and reading lives. The window in which formal schooling expects reading to occur is much narrower than the window in which children actually learn it on their own terms.

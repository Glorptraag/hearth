## Text

The term "strewing" was coined in unschooling circles in the 1990s by Sandra Dodd. It names a specific practice the movement had been doing for years without a word for it: the casual, unannounced placement of interesting things — books, materials, browser tabs, art supplies, museum brochures, half-built projects — within the child's natural path, with no instruction attached and no expectation of engagement. Authentic strewing is sharing genuine enthusiasm rather than disguising curriculum. The child who ignores the strewn item has given a complete and valid response.

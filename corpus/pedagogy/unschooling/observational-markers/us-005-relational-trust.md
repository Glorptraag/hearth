## What it indicates

Because unschooling depends on the child trusting the parent enough to let them in, and the parent trusting the child enough to follow rather than direct, the quality of the relationship is itself the most important capability the approach develops. The unschooling tradition treats relational trust not as an outcome of education but as the medium through which education happens. If the trust is weakening, the approach is in trouble regardless of what activities are or are not happening.

## Look for

- Child volunteers what they are thinking, doing, or struggling with
- Child seeks out the parent for conversation, not only for needs
- Child is honest about things they got wrong or did badly
- Child can disagree with the parent without distress
- Parent and child have inside jokes, shared references, and fluent conversation
- The parent does not feel they are losing track of who the child is

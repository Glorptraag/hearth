## Text

In a survey of 232 families practising unschooling, the most commonly cited challenge was not the children's learning but the parents' own inner work — overcoming feelings of social criticism, navigating disapproval from extended family, and releasing internalised assumptions about what learning is supposed to look like. The challenges of the method live more in the parent's nervous system than in the child's progress.

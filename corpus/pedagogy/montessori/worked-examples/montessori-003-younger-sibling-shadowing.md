## Scenario

"Theo (3) insisted on sitting next to Isla (6) while she did her reading work this morning. He had his own tray with the colour tablets and matched them quietly for about 20 minutes. He didn't interrupt her once."

## Interpretation

Mixed-age environment working as intended. Theo's developmental need is sensorial (colour tablets are exactly right for his stage); Isla's is academic. Placing them in the same working atmosphere with stage-appropriate work for each honours both cycles. Twenty minutes of colour matching is a strong concentration marker for a 3-year-old.

What to notice next: Theo may be ready for more sensorial graduation — pink tower, brown stair, or more challenging colour grading. Introduce one new material this week.

Science English

Little Nature Scientists

Five interconnected modules that take children from close observation to pattern recognition to scientific classification. Designed for Australian backyards, local parks, and bush walks. Children develop real scientific habits of mind through hands-on exploration of their natural environment.

📦 5 modules
👤 Ages 5–7
📅 2+ weeks of content
🧑‍🔬
Dr. Sarah Chen ✓
Wildlife Educator · 15 years field experience · Former CSIRO outreach
💡
Suggestion: The module "Patterns in Nature" builds on observation skills. You might enjoy "Observing Closely" first — but it's not required.
Modules in this pack
1
Observing Closely
Foundation module. Slow observation, magnifying glass exploration, and nature drawings. Always unlocked.
🔍 5 approaches ⏱️ 15–30 min
2
Patterns in Nature
Spotting repeating shapes, symmetry in leaves, and spiral patterns. Builds on observation skills from Module 1.
🔍 4 approaches ⏱️ 20–30 min
3
Living vs Non-Living
Classification fundamentals. Sorting, grouping, and explaining reasoning. Develops scientific language.
🔍 4 approaches ⏱️ 15–25 min
4
Minibeasts
Insect observation, habitat investigation, and lifecycle understanding through direct experience with invertebrates.
🔍 5 approaches ⏱️ 20–40 min
5
Nature Scientist Project
Capstone. Children design and conduct their own nature investigation, applying all skills learned. Badge earned here.
🎓 Capstone ⏱️ 30–45 min
